Mechanistic Drivers of Sycophancy in Autoregressive Transformers

A 20-Question Empirical Manifesto, the Dual-Circuit Suppression Discovery, and the Extended TransformerLens Toolkit

Investigates the causal mechanisms behind sycophantic alignment failure in autoregressive language models (pythia-1.4b). Disproves the classical parametric retrieval failure hypothesis (clean MLP activation recovery yields only 5.8%), demonstrating that factual associations are successfully retrieved into the residual stream but suppressed downstream. Discovers a concentrated dual-circuit suppression mechanism in Layers 13–15 and demonstrates zero-shot inference-time surgical ablation to reverse sycophancy while preserving 94.2% baseline factual confidence.
